Delete your Tally account

Last updated: November 19, 2026

Tally by Ranova Labs has no account unless you turn on co-parent sharing. This page explains how to delete your data and, if you used sharing, your sign-in.

How to delete

  1. Open Tally on your iPhone, iPad, or Android phone.
  2. Go to Settings.
  3. Tap Delete account, then confirm.

What is deleted

  • On your device: every child, logged moment, goal, redemption, and photo.
  • If you used co-parent sharing: if you are the owner, the shared copy of your household is removed from our servers (Google Firebase) and every co-parent keeps their own local copy. If you joined as a co-parent, you leave the household and your membership is removed. Your sign-in (Sign in with Apple or Google) record is deleted in both cases.
  • If you never turned sharing on: we hold nothing about you, so there is nothing stored on our side to delete.

What may remain

  • iPhone and iPad with iCloud sync on: the deletion syncs to your iCloud account.
  • Android: your Google device backup is replaced the next time your phone backs up, or you can remove it in your Google account's backup settings.
  • Purchase records for Tally Plus are kept by Apple or Google and our subscription provider, RevenueCat, as required for billing.

Can't open the app?

Email ranovalabs@icloud.com from the address linked to your account and we will delete your shared data and sign-in for you.
