Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 8, 2026

Tally is built to work without collecting data about you or your family beyond what the app needs to function. This page explains exactly what that means.

This policy covers Tally on iPhone, iPad, and Android.

What Tally stores

The behavior, reward, and household information you enter — including any proof photos you attach to a moment — is stored on your device. We do not have access to it, cannot see it, and do not store a copy of it on any server we operate.

  • iPhone and iPad: if you enable iCloud sync, a copy is also kept in your own private iCloud account.
  • Android: if backup is turned on in your device settings, Android includes Tally's data in your device backup in your Google account, so it can be restored on a new phone. That backup is managed by Google under your account; we cannot access it.

Backup and export files you create in Tally's Settings go only where you choose to save or share them.

Permissions

Tally asks for access only when you use a feature that needs it:

  • Notifications — for the daily reminder and weekly summary. These are scheduled on your device; no server sends them.
  • Camera and photos — only when you add a proof photo to a moment. On Android, Tally uses the system camera and photo picker, so it never gets access to the rest of your photo library.
  • Face ID, Touch ID, fingerprint, or screen lock — for the optional App Lock and for unlocking Kid Mode if you forget its PIN. Your device performs the check; Tally never receives your biometric data.

Purchases

Tally Plus subscriptions are processed by Apple (App Store) or Google (Google Play), depending on where you subscribe, and by RevenueCat, our subscription infrastructure provider. RevenueCat receives an anonymous app user ID plus the purchase and device identifiers needed to validate your subscription. We never receive your payment details. See RevenueCat's privacy policy.

Crash reports

On iPhone and iPad, Tally uses Firebase Crashlytics to learn when the app crashes so we can fix it. If the app crashes, technical details (what the app was doing, device model, OS version) are sent to Firebase (Google). This is not linked to your identity and is never used for advertising or tracking. See Firebase's privacy policy.

The Android app does not include a crash-reporting service. If you have allowed it in your device settings, Google Play may share anonymous, aggregated crash and performance statistics with us.

What we don't do

  • No advertising, and no ad tracking.
  • No selling or sharing your data with third parties for marketing.

Deleting your data

Settings › Delete account removes every child, logged moment, goal, redemption, and photo from your device; uninstalling Tally removes them too. On iPhone and iPad with iCloud sync on, the deletion also syncs to your iCloud account. On Android, your Google device backup is replaced the next time your phone backs up, or you can remove it in your Google account's backup settings. Because we never hold your data, there is nothing stored on our side to delete.

Children's privacy

Tally is designed for use by parents and guardians. Any information about a child (name, logged behavior) is entered by the parent, stored as described above, and never collected by us directly from a child.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the "Last updated" date at the top of this page will change too. Continued use of Tally after an update means you accept the revised policy.
